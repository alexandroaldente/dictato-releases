# Dictato Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Dictato is designed for local voice dictation on macOS.

## What Dictato accesses

- **Microphone:** Dictato records only while you start dictation. The temporary
  audio file is processed locally and removed after transcription or when the
  recording is cancelled.
- **Input Monitoring and Accessibility:** these macOS permissions are optional.
  They are used only for a global push-to-talk shortcut and for inserting the
  resulting text into the currently active app.
- **Clipboard:** when automatic paste is enabled, Dictato temporarily uses the
  clipboard to paste the dictated text, then restores the previous clipboard
  contents where macOS permits.
- **Local model files:** after you confirm it, Dictato downloads a speech model
  to your Mac. The download request goes to the model host, but your recorded
  audio is not uploaded to that host.

## What Dictato does not do

- It does not create an account or require an API key.
- It does not send dictated audio or transcription text to Dictato servers.
- It does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
- It does not keep a speech history.

Dictato may check the public GitHub release feed for a newer app version. That
request contains no dictated audio or transcription text.

## Local storage

Dictato stores your chosen settings, downloaded speech models, and diagnostic
information on your Mac. Diagnostic logs do not contain your transcribed text.
You can remove the application and its support files at any time.

## Changes and contact

Updates to this policy are published with Dictato releases. For privacy
questions or deletion help, open an issue in the Dictato releases repository.
