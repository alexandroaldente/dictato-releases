# Dictato 0.1.2 Alpha

- The macOS microphone permission appears immediately when you first start
  dictation, even while the local recognition model is preparing.
- Local model downloads now require clear confirmation and show their size.
- Added a privacy policy inside the DMG and a Privacy link in About.
- Audio remains on the Mac; temporary recordings are removed after use.

This is an Apple Silicon alpha build. It is not yet notarized by Apple.
